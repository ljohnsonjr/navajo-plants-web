Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Overview

Navajo Plants ("the App") is a plant identification and learning reference. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No account or login is required.

Local Storage

The App stores your preferences (such as favorited plants) locally on your device using standard browser or device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with or send data to any third-party services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you may contact the developer through the Google Play store listing.
