About Diné Ch'il

This free preview contains 50 of 199 plants found across the Navajo Nation. Get the full app with all plants, audio pronunciations by a native Diné speaker, and offline support.

How to Use

  • Search — Type any Navajo, English, or scientific name. Filter by plant type using the chips below the search bar.
  • Browse — Explore plants grouped by type: grasses, shrubs, trees, forbs, cacti, and grasslike plants.
  • Details — Tap any plant to see its full information including habitat, growing season, and special notes.
  • Favorites — Tap the heart icon to save plants for quick reference.

Data

Plant data compiled for educational and cultural preservation purposes. Navajo names reflect traditional naming by Diné communities.
